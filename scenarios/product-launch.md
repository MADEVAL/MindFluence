# Scenario: Product Launch

> **See also:** `SKILL.md` → Bias Catalog for full mechanism & application of each bias.
> **Anti-patterns:** `SKILL.md` → Anti-Patterns section for detection rules.
> **Cultural:** `SKILL.md` → Cultural Quick-Reference for region-specific adjustments.

## CONTEXT
You are planning a product launch - from pre-launch hype through launch day to post-launch follow-up.

## GOAL
Build anticipation, convert early adopters, and maximize launch-day sales while building a base of evangelists.

---

## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH (7-14 days before)

### Bias Stack
`Fear (#5, problem awareness) → Authority (#4, why we built this) → Social Proof (#1, waitlist) → In-Group (#15, early access)`

> ⚠ **AP-11:** Stories need ≥3 concrete sensory details. **AP-2:** Every fear trigger must include a solution exit in the same paragraph. **AP-1:** Social proof requires specific, verifiable numbers. **AP-8:** In-group needs a named identity + shared experience + out-group.

### Content Types
1. **Problem Awareness Posts** - Vividly describe the pain. Do NOT mention the product. Make the problem feel urgent and personal.
   - *Primary bias: Availability Heuristic (#6)* - tell a specific story of someone struggling with this problem.
   - *Secondary: Fear/Loss Aversion (#5)* - "This is costing you $X per [day/week/month]."

2. **Origin Story** - Why you're building this. Personal, vulnerable, mission-driven.
   - *Primary bias: Authority (#4)* - establish founder/product credibility.
   - *Secondary: In-Group Favoritism (#15)* - "We're building this for people like us who..."

3. **Waitlist / Early Access** - Open a waitlist. Share numbers as they grow.
   - *Primary bias: Social Proof (#1)* - "2,147 people already on the list."
   - *Secondary: Scarcity (→ #5)* - "First 500 get lifetime discount."
   - *Tertiary: Endowment Effect (#10)* - give waitlist members something now (early content, preview, name in credits).

4. **Behind-the-Scenes** - Show the building process. Humanize the product.
   - *Primary bias: Halo Effect (#16)* - the quality and care you put in transfers to perceived product quality.

---

## PHASE 2: LAUNCH DAY

### Bias Stack
`Urgency / Scarcity (#5) → Anchoring (#2, pricing) → Social Proof (#1, live purchases) → Risk Reversal (guarantee)`

> ⚠ **AP-6:** Scarcity must be genuine and explained (why is it limited?). **AP-3:** Framing needs a concrete, quantified anchor. **AP-4:** Authority claims need named sources. **AP-7:** Sunk cost is late-stage only - don't invoke for first-time buyers.

### Content Types
1. **Launch Announcement** - The big reveal. Frame as the solution to the problem you've been building up.
   - *Primary bias: Framing (#3)* - "This isn't [category]. This is [new category you're creating]."
   - *Secondary: Authority (#4)* - cite the problem research, your credentials, the build process.

2. **Pricing Reveal** - Use anchoring aggressively.
   - Show "what it should cost" (the anchor) → show the actual price.
   - Limited-time launch discount.
   - Bonus stack: add bonuses with assigned dollar values to inflate perceived value.

3. **Social Proof Wave** - Pre-seed testimonials from beta users. Share purchase numbers live.
   - "Just crossed 500 sales in the first hour."

4. **Urgency Injection** - Countdown, limited bonuses, price increase warning.
   - *Primary bias: Loss Aversion (#5)* - "Price goes up in 24 hours. Bonus package disappears."
   - *Secondary: Sunk Cost (#12)* for those who've been following the pre-launch.

---

## PHASE 3: POST-LAUNCH (3-7 days after)

### Bias Stack
`Confirmation (#7, you made the right choice) → Social Proof (#1, look who else bought) → In-Group (#15, welcome to the community)`

> ⚠ **AP-12:** Dissonance resolution must blame the system, not the person. **AP-10:** Confirmation should make the reader feel smart, not foolish. **AP-8:** Community activation needs a named in-group identity.

### Content Types
1. **Purchase Justification** - Content that helps buyers feel smart about their decision.
   - Case studies, "what to expect" guides, setup tutorials.
   - *Primary bias: Cognitive Dissonance resolution (#8)* - buyers seek evidence they chose right.

2. **Community Welcoming** - Activate the in-group.
   - "Welcome to the 3,200+ who've joined."
   - Private community access.
   - *Primary bias: In-Group Favoritism (#15) + Group Polarization (#20)* - shared identity strengthens loyalty.

3. **Last-Chance Scarcity** - Final call before price increase / bonus removal.
   - "Doors closing. Last chance at this price."

4. **User-Generated Content Prompt** - Ask new users to share. Their confirmation bias becomes your marketing.

---

## BIAS CHECKLIST FOR LAUNCH
- [ ] Problem made vivid and personal before product revealed
- [ ] Origin story establishes authority and relatability
- [ ] Pricing anchored against higher reference point
- [ ] Social proof visible at every stage (numbers, faces, testimonials)
- [ ] Urgency mechanisms genuine (not fake scarcity)
- [ ] Risk reversed (guarantee, trial, easy cancellation)
- [ ] Post-purchase content feeds confirmation bias (#7)
- [ ] Community element (in-group) activated immediately after purchase
